Welcome to Shivesh Castings

We are a well-established firm engaged in the manufacturing and supplying of graded gray iron castings, with our foundry located at Belgaum and a staff strength of 25 people. We offer a wide range of grey iron castings for engineering products and components used in industries manufacturing valves, pulleys, couplings, crankshafts, helical rotors, counterweights, machine tools, automotive components, and more. We provide customized solutions and package consignments as per client specifications, and under the guidance of our Managing Director, Mr. Maruti Godse, our efficient team and advanced machinery help us meet bulk market demands across our vast client network throughout the country.

Quality is our prime focus, with quality auditors supervising every stage of production and conducting various tests to ensure supreme product quality. We are certified with ISO 9001:2015 from ICS, and our well-equipped manufacturing unit enables us to meet the huge demands of our clients.

OUR DEPARTMENTS

Moulding Department

The moulding department is responsible for shaping sand moulds that form the foundation of the casting process. Using controlled sand preparation and moulding techniques, this department ensures accurate dimensions and structural integrity of castings. Proper moulding directly impacts product quality, surface finish, and consistency.

Core Shop

The core shop plays a vital role in producing internal cavities and complex shapes within castings. Precision-made cores are prepared using high-quality sand and binders to ensure strength and accuracy during metal pouring. This department supports the manufacturing of intricate components used in engineering and automotive applications.

Melting Department

The melting department handles the controlled melting of grey iron using induction furnaces. Strict temperature control and chemical composition monitoring ensure the molten metal meets required specifications. This department is critical for maintaining material quality, consistency, and mechanical properties of the final castings.

Fettling Department

Fettling involves cleaning and finishing cast components after solidification. This department removes excess material, gates, risers, and surface irregularities using grinding and cutting tools. Proper fettling improves appearance, dimensional accuracy, and readiness for further machining or direct use.

Pattern Shop

The pattern shop designs and maintains patterns used for mould preparation. Accurate patterns are essential for achieving precise dimensions and repeatable quality. This department supports both standard and customized casting requirements, enabling flexibility in production.

Quality Control Department

The quality control department ensures that every casting meets defined quality standards. Through inspections, testing, and process monitoring, this department verifies dimensional accuracy, hardness, and material composition. Continuous quality checks help prevent defects and ensure consistent product performance.

Maintenance Department

The maintenance department ensures smooth operation of all machinery and equipment. Regular preventive maintenance minimizes breakdowns, improves efficiency, and extends equipment life. This department plays a crucial role in maintaining uninterrupted production and workplace safety.

OUR INFRASTRUCTURE

1

Induction Furnace (150 & 300 Kgs)

The induction furnaces enable efficient and controlled melting of grey iron. Their capacity supports both small and medium batch production while ensuring uniform metal quality and energy efficiency.

2

Bench Grinder

Bench grinders are used for finishing operations such as edge smoothing and surface cleaning. They help achieve proper dimensional accuracy and improved surface finish during the fettling process.

3

Sand Muller

The sand muller ensures uniform mixing of sand, binders, and additives. Proper sand preparation enhances mould strength, surface finish, and casting accuracy.

4

Pin Lift Moulding Machine

This machine supports efficient and consistent mould production. It improves productivity and ensures uniform compaction of sand moulds, leading to better casting quality.

5

Spectrometer

The spectrometer is used to analyze the chemical composition of molten metal. It ensures that material properties meet required specifications, helping maintain consistent casting quality.

6

Microscope

Microscopic analysis helps examine metal structure and detect defects. This equipment supports quality assurance and metallurgical analysis.

7

Hardness Tester

Microscopic analysis helps examine metal structure and detect defects. This equipment supports quality assurance and metallurgical analysis.

8

Pyrometer

The pyrometer measures molten metal temperature accurately. Proper temperature control is critical to avoiding defects and ensuring sound castings.

9

Generator

The generator provides backup power to ensure uninterrupted production during power outages, maintaining delivery commitments and operational efficiency.

10

CE Meter

The CE meter helps monitor carbon equivalent levels in molten iron, ensuring correct material properties and reducing casting defects.

11

Shot Blasting Machine

Shot blasting cleans casting surfaces by removing sand and scale. This process improves surface finish and prepares components for machining or dispatch.

12

Sand Crusher Machine

The sand crusher recycles used sand, reducing material wastage and ensuring consistent sand quality for repeated moulding operations.
